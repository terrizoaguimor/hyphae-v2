\begin{abstract}
We introduce \hyphae, a cognitive substrate that answers retrieval
queries by emitting byte-identical quotations of stored memory
fragments, joined by a curated connective lexicon, with no large
language model (LLM) in the cognition path. \hyphae{}'s
distinguishing property is \emph{architectural verifiability}:
every quoted span is preserved byte-for-byte against a SHA-256
hash-chained journal, so any output can be independently confirmed
to have been assembled from named, unaltered source fragments --- a
guarantee no retrieval-augmented LLM provides by construction,
because generation paraphrases its sources. We evaluate against 18
LLM-based configurations (vanilla RAG, HyDE plus cross-encoder
reranking, oracle context) over six generator models (from
Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, two from Meta, and an in-house
router; $6\times3 = 18$ configurations), plus a
trivial \textbf{echo baseline} that emits the retrieved sentence
verbatim with no model and no lexicon. The echo baseline is the
critical control, and its result reframes the contribution: on
standard correctness and NLI-grounding metrics (gold-answer match,
unsupported-claim rate) echo \emph{ties or exceeds} \hyphae{} on
both a 150-query TriviaQA sample and a 34-query composition
corpus. Measured correctness is therefore a property of verbatim
quotation itself --- shared by any echo --- not of \hyphae{}'s
composition machinery; our ablations independently confirm that
the lexicon, cascade-shape composition, and boundary smoothing do
not move the correctness metrics. What echo lacks, and what no
metric in the standard benchmark captures, is verifiable
provenance: echo emits text with no audit relation to a
tamper-evident store. We therefore position \hyphae{} not as a
more-correct system but as a \emph{correctness-neutral} one that
adds cryptographic provenance and runs in microseconds on
commodity CPU --- five-to-six orders of magnitude below LLM
inference, a corollary of not generating rather than a result in
itself. We argue the durable contribution is the architectural
audit property, that the standard correctness benchmark cannot
measure it (a trivial echo saturates it), and that evaluating
verifiable generation requires provenance-centric tasks the
current literature does not provide. Code, corpora, result
envelopes (including the echo control), and this paper's source
are at \url{https://github.com/terrizoaguimor/hyphae-v2}.
\end{abstract}
