\begin{abstract}
We introduce \hyphae, a cognitive substrate that composes responses
by verbatim quotation of retrieved memory fragments stitched
together with a curated connective lexicon, with no large language
model (LLM) in the cognition path. The architectural commitment
underlying the system is dual: every quoted body is preserved
byte-identically against a hash-chained substrate journal, making
each emitted response \emph{independently verifiable} against
named source material; and the realizer's bounded output runs in
microseconds on a single CPU core. We compare \hyphae{} against
18 LLM-based configurations (vanilla retrieval-augmented generation,
HyDE plus cross-encoder reranking, and oracle context) spanning
five frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, and Meta,
across two corpora: the project's own 34-query composition corpus,
and 150 random samples from TriviaQA. We report two
complementary metrics: NLI-grounded
unsupported-claim rate (does the response stay inside the retrieved
context?) and gold-answer match (does the response contain the
benchmark's reference answer?). On TriviaQA, \hyphae{} reaches both
$1.000$ gold-answer match and a degenerate-zero unsupported-claim
rate explained mechanically by the metric's filter interacting with
the substrate's compositional template; the best LLM-based
configuration reaches $0.960$ gold-answer match but $0.400$--$0.665$
NLI unsupported-claim. The honest reading is that frontier LLMs are
\emph{nearly as correct} on this corpus and \emph{less efficiently
grounded} by the chosen metric. Where the architectural claim is
unambiguous is the cost dimension: \hyphae{}'s realizer runs in
$0.002$ ms versus the hosted LLM's $2{,}424$ ms (six orders of
magnitude); against a local Llama-8B-Instruct quantised baseline on
identical CPU hardware the ratio is approximately $10^5$ rather
than $10^6$ but the production economics still differ by
$\sim 4{-}5$ orders of magnitude. We do not claim the substrate
universally replaces LLM-based generation. We claim that for tasks
in which the unit of grounding is a discrete retrieved fragment
quoted verbatim and the deployment requires audit-bound
verifiability, the architectural commitment is competitive with
frontier LLM correctness and dominates them on latency, memory
footprint, and per-query cost by several orders of magnitude. Code,
corpora, result envelopes, and hardware traces are available at
\url{https://github.com/terrizoaguimor/hyphae-v2}.
\end{abstract}
